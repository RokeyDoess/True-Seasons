# Community guidelines

Keep issues and pull requests respectful and focused on the project. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination and threats are not.

Do not publish another person's private information. Do not spam, advertise unrelated projects or repeatedly derail a conversation.

Report problematic content using GitHub's reporting tools. The maintainer may remove content, close conversations or restrict participation when needed. These guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and reviews.
